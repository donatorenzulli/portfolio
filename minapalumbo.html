Museo Naturalistico Minà Palumbo

At the Minà Palumbo Natural History Museum, we did it through an immersive 3D diorama of the Madonie Park—one of the most biodiverse areas in the Mediterranean.
I worked on the 3D scans and the digital reconstruction, recreating a virtual environment that mirrors the Park’s atmosphere and terrain. Sounds, lights, and images guide visitors through its extraordinary life forms—from ancient trees to rare species that make this landscape a living wonder once studied by Francesco Minà Palumbo.
