Il gioco del FAFA FEST

We made a video game for Fafa Fest 26 and called it ‘The Fafa Fest Game’. During Fafa Camp, we wrote the story and learnt how to use a button. Then we met up again and the rest just fell into place. The rest consists of: 5 little animals, 5 levels, 5 backgrounds, 5 obstacles, 1 red button, various cables, 3 televisions, around 300 participants, and more than 10,000 red stars collected collaboratively by everyone at the festival. All this with a single aim: to make the FAFA FEST Translated with DeepL.com (free version)
