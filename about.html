about

Hello! I'm Donato Renzulli, a 25-year-old communication designer currently based in Milan. I specialize in exploring new technologies to craft contemporary visual languages that resonate with today’s world. I hold both a bachelor’s and a master’s degree in Communication Design from Politecnico di Milano. During my studies, I had the enriching opportunity to take part in two Erasmus programs—first at IADE in Lisbon, and later at Aalto University in Helsinki. Over the years, I’ve developed a strong passion for using technology as a creative tool to make sense of the complex world we live in—by turning it into something visually compelling and meaningful.

One of the projects I am particularly proud of is Generact, an association we founded with friends from my city (Foggia, Puglia) with which we try to propose active citizenship by redeveloping abandoned and underused spaces through cultural events. Check it out!
