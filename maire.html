Maire | Depth cam visual

For this DJ set, I created real-time visuals that reacted to the audience’s presence. Using a depth camera pointed at the crowd, the visuals changed based on people’s movement and proximity—turning spectators into active elements of the performance.
The project was developed in collaboration with UniversityBox for Maire, an energy company, combining technology, interaction, and live music into one immersive experience.
